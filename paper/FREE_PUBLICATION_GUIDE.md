## 🏆 Top Platforms to Publish Your Research Paper for 100% FREE

In Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, the world's top research is published on **Free Open-Access Preprint Repositories** and **Diamond Open-Access Journals / Peer-Reviewed Conferences** (which charge ₹0 / \$0 to authors).

Below are the best platforms, ordered by recommendation:

---

### 1. arXiv.org (Primary Recommendation — Global Gold Standard)
* **Website:** [https://arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org)
* **Cost:** **100% FREE** ($0 / ₹0)
* **Turnaround:** Live within 24 to 48 hours.
* **Categories:** `cs.AI` (Artificial Intelligence), `cs.SE` (Software Engineering), or `cs.DC` (Distributed, Parallel, and Cluster Computing).
* **Benefits:**
  - Standard platform used by Google DeepMind, OpenAI, Stanford, MIT, and IITs.
  - Automatically indexed by **Google Scholar**, Semantic Scholar, and DBLP.
  - Generates an official, citable identifier (e.g., `arXiv:2610.XXXXX`).
  - Recognized worldwide on resumes, PhD applications, and academic grants.
* **Requirements:** Requires a free account. First-time submitters to a category need an endorsement from an existing arXiv author (your university professor or mentor can endorse with 1 click).

---

### 2. TechRxiv (By IEEE — Fastest 100% Free IEEE Preprint)
* **Website:** [https://www.techrxiv.org](https://www.techrxiv.org)
* **Cost:** **100% FREE**
* **Turnaround:** 24–48 hours after basic moderation.
* **Domain:** Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI & Autonomous Systems.
* **Benefits:**
  - Backed directly by **IEEE** (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers).
  - Assigns an immediate, permanent **citable DOI** (Digital Object Identifier).
  - No endorsement needed; simple registration with your university or personal email.
  - Fully compatible with future submission to IEEE conferences or journals (IEEE allows TechRxiv preprints).

---

### 3. Zenodo (By CERN & European Commission)
* **Website:** [https://zenodo.org](https://zenodo.org)
* **Cost:** **100% FREE**
* **Turnaround:** **Instant (Immediate publication upon submit)**.
* **Benefits:**
  - Hosted on CERN’s ultra-reliable global infrastructure.
  - Gives an official **CERN/Datacite DOI** instantly.
  - You can upload both the **Paper PDF** and the **Code Zip** together, creating an immutable scientific record.

---

### 4. Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR) via OpenReview
* **Website:** [https://openreview.net/group?id=TMLR](https://openreview.net/group?id=TMLR)
* **Cost:** **100% FREE (Diamond Open Access)**
* **Type:** Full Peer-Reviewed Academic Journal.
* **Review Cycle:** Rolling submissions year-round (decision in ~6–8 weeks).
* **Benefits:**
  - Top-tier machine learning journal run by leading researchers.
  - Completely double-blind or public review on OpenReview.
  - No Article Processing Charges (APCs).

---

### 5. PeerJ Computer Science
* **Website:** [https://peerj.com/computer-science/](https://peerj.com/computer-science/)
* **Cost:** Free submission; waiver options for students from India/developing regions.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Instructions: How to Compile & Submit Your Paper

### Step 1: Open the LaTeX Paper in Overleaf (Free)
1. Go to **[https://www.overleaf.com](https://www.overleaf.com)** and create a free account (or log in with Google).
2. Click **New Project** $\rightarrow$ **Blank Project** $\rightarrow$ Name it `SADR_Research_Paper`.
3. In your project, upload the following files from your repository's `paper/` folder:
   - `paper/main.tex`
   - `paper/references.bib`
   - `paper/figure1_token_complexity.png`
   - `paper/figure2_drift_control.png`
4. Click the green **Recompile** button.
5. You will see the beautiful, 2-column IEEE/ACM styled research paper.
6. Click **Download PDF** to save the publication-ready PDF on your computer.

---

### Step 2: Upload to TechRxiv (Recommended for Instant Free DOI)
1. Go to **[https://www.techrxiv.org/submit](https://www.techrxiv.org/submit)**.
2. Sign in with your email or IEEE account.
3. Fill in the metadata:
   - **Title:** `State-Aware Durable Runtime: A Governed Execution Engine for Reliable Long-Horizon AI Agents`
   - **Authors:** Nishita Singh (Department of Computer Science and Engineering)
   - **Subject Area:** Computer Science $\rightarrow$ Artificial Intelligence / Software Engineering
   - **Abstract:** Copy and paste the Abstract from `paper/RESEARCH_PAPER.md`.
   - **Keywords:** `Autonomous AI Agents, Long-Horizon Planning, State Management, Behavioral Contracts, Anti-Fabrication, Fault Tolerance`
4. Upload your compiled PDF.
5. Under License, choose **CC BY 4.0** (standard Creative Commons Attribution).
6. Click **Submit**. Within 24–48 hours, your paper will be officially published with an IEEE TechRxiv DOI!
